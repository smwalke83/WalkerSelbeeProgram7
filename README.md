# WalkerSelbeeProgram7  
Group Project for C++ II - BlackJack Game  
  
This was a collaboration between Scott Walker and Rikki Selbee for CNM's C++ II course. It is a blackjack game. Here are the rules (taken from the rules menu in the program):  
  
OBJECTIVE  
Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.  
  
CARD VALUES  
Number cards are worth their face value.  
Jack, Queen, and King are worth 10.  
An Ace is worth 1 or 11, whichever helps your hand more.  
    
HOW TO PLAY  
1. Enter a bet and press Place Bet.  
2. You and the dealer each get two cards. One dealer card is hidden.  
3. Press Hit to take another card, or Stand to keep your hand.  
4. If you go over 21, you bust and lose your bet.  
5. When you stand, the dealer reveals the hidden card and must hit  
until reaching 17 or more.  
  
PAYOUTS  
Win: you win the amount of your bet.  
BlackJack (Ace + 10-point card on the deal): you win 1.5 x your bet.  
Tie: no money changes hands.  
Lose: you lose your bet.  
  
Press Play Again to start a new hand.
